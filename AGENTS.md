## 4. Art direction — reference first, not generic AI UI
Use the team's supplied dark gaming-interface screenshot as the composition reference: near-black stage, quietly aligned header, centered substantial white headline, short muted supporting text, single action, subtle nearly invisible grid receding behind the hero, five colorful overlapping rounded cards at the bottom. The central cyan card is largest and foregrounded; adjacent coral/mint cards and outer purple/indigo cards are offset and slightly rotated. Replace game artwork with original simple SVG geometry. Do not copy Nintendo branding, logos, characters, account/currency controls, rankings, or fake game navigation.

A designer should see one strong composition, not a collection of template sections. The cards themselves carry most of the color. Use dark neutral background (#101115), surface (#1B1D25), raised surface (#282B35), text (#F5F5F7), readable muted text (#A6A8B5), cyan (#26C7F5), purple (#7C54F5), coral (#FF626C), mint (#26D6AA), orange (#FF9C48). Indigo for square artwork may use #4658D9. Colors are accents, not instructions to add glow. Prefer Sora or a strong system sans-serif for the headline and UI; Michroma is optional for a restrained wordmark only if available. Use sentence/title case and natural tracking. One strong h1, one concise tagline, one short description, one clear CTA. No duplicate marketing copy. Strong typography and whitespace are the design.

### Non-negotiable anti-generic-design rules
- No purple/cyan radial glow wallpaper, neon text shadows, glowing borders or glowing CTA. Remove these existing W01 effects in W02, not by rerunning W01.
- No glassmorphism everywhere, backdrop-filter blur, floating orb decorations, artificial dashboard metrics, gratuitous badges, icon clouds, decorative eyebrow labels, excessive uppercase/letter spacing, generic bento/card grids, or gradients on every component.
- No oversized pill buttons (use 10–14px radius and a controlled border/shadow); no fake 3D bevel overload. Keep actual interactive targets at least 44px.
- Use near-solid neutral surfaces and a faint low-contrast grid; cards may have one modest same-hue gradient and one subtle shadow. Keep vivid colors on artwork, selected/feedback states, and limited actions. Use 16–24px radii on artwork; do not wrap every text block in a card.
- Layout: restrained horizontal header, centered hero with generous but measured whitespace, cards positioned as one intentional fan, not five separate feature tiles. Avoid overlap with CTA. On mobile reduce/contain decorative artwork without clipping controls or text. No decorative motion while idle.
- Motion: 150–250ms hover/press/fade, no continuous bouncing, no arbitrary stagger or autoplay, honor prefers-reduced-motion. No animation that obstructs feedback.
- Text: remove redundant promotional copy only in an assigned HTML task. No filler like "unlock your potential", "experience the future", or "level up your journey". Use concise functional labels and plain language.
- Check actual screenshots at 1440, 768, 360px when available; compare composition with the supplied reference, not a generic UI kit. State explicitly if browser/screenshot testing is unavailable. Do not invent test results.

## 6. Screen design
Landing: centered original wordmark/title/tagline and Start the Quiz, with the five-card geometry fan beneath. No fake navigation. Quiz: one calm game canvas, compact progress and score, large crisp SVG, legible question, four tactile numeric buttons in 2×2, visible correct/wrong states and clear Next. Desktop two-column, mobile stacked. No extra panels, progress badges, or invented difficulty/timer. Feedback: one concise message, relevant GIF if available, correct answer on wrong; no giant glow or confetti. Results: prominent score, short message, Play Again; no fake trophy statistics.

## 7. JavaScript contract
Use readable functions: randomNumber(min,max), randomItem(items), shuffleArray(items), generateShape(), drawShape(shape), generateQuestion(shape), generateOptions(answer), startQuiz(), showQuestion(), checkAnswer(value), nextQuestion(), showResults(), restartQuiz(), showRandomMeme(isCorrect).
Geometry object: type polygon/star, count, vertices [{x,y}], facts {sides,corners,angles,outerTips (star only)}. Polygon 3–10 sides; simple non-self-intersecting star 4–8 outer tips with alternating inner/outer radii, 2× tips outline sides/corners/angles. Fixed SVG viewBox, Math.sin/cos. Same geometry drives rendering and answer. Ask count of sides, corners, angles, or star outer tips; not angle degrees. Use plain loops/functions, descriptive names, short useful comments.
TOTAL_QUESTIONS=10, currentQuestion, score, activeQuestion, answered. Four distinct positive numeric options, exactly one correct, finite candidate pool, Fisher–Yates shuffle. Numeric compare, lock answers, one score increment, reveal correct answer on wrong, Next after answer, final score and restart reset. Use correctMemes/wrongMemes arrays only with approved direct URLs. Empty or failed URLs hide image but preserve text; ignore stale callbacks. No fabricated URLs.
